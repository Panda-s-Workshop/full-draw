Bestiary

Creatures

Three creatures that each ask for a different shot, and the Marksman.

Shieldbearer

A pillager's cousin behind a tall iron-banded tower shield, walking at you behind it. It joins raids from wave 3 and guards pillager outposts. Up close it bashes with the shield.

Health
30
Armour
6
Shield bash
5

How to beat it

  • The shield stops every arrow within 60° of where it faces. Circle it: it turns slowly, and a sprinting player out-circles it within about six blocks.
  • Arrows that come down at 35° or steeper drop in over the top.
  • A Blunt Arrow on the shield knocks it aside for 1.5 s; an Arbalest bolt cracks it down for 3 s.

Drops

  • Emerald ×0–1 (killed by a player)
  • Iron Nugget ×1–3

Gale Harrier

A large pale raptor with storm-blue wingtips. It circles 20–30 blocks up by day over windswept hills, windswept gravelly hills, windswept forest, jagged peaks, frozen peaks and stony peaks, then shrieks, folds its wings and dives straight at you.

Health
24
Dive
6

How to beat it

  • The shriek and the folded wings are your second to aim. During the dive it is in reach of an arrow.
  • A Perfect Release or a headshot during the dive grounds it for 3 s, and a grounded Harrier takes ×2 from melee.
  • Out of a dive it climbs back out of a sword's reach.

Drops

  • Harrier Feather ×1–3
  • Harrier Talon ×0–1

Bog Lurker

A mossy, frog-like ambusher in the water of swamp and mangrove swamp, by day and by night, only its two glowing yellow eyes above the surface. From 8–16 blocks it spits a glob that does 2 and slows you for 3 s.

Health
18
Spit
2

How to beat it

  • Its eyes are its weak spot and take ×2. A body hit through the water does half.
  • Come within 4 blocks and it sinks and swims away, so keep your distance and shoot.

Drops

  • Bog Eye ×0–1
  • Slimeball ×0–2

The Marksman

A hooded skeletal archer that keeps the top of a Ranger's Watchtower. It steps into a crenel's gap, draws with a loud creak, looses a Perfect shot led ahead of you, then ducks behind the parapet and moves to another. A boss bar shows its health.

Health
100
Armour
4
Bow's end
3

How to beat it

  • The creak is the warning: take cover when you hear it, and shoot when it stands in the gap to draw.
  • Ducked behind the parapet only its head shows. Arrows into the stone fall away.
  • Below half health it draws faster (1.2 s instead of 1.5 s), lights up a hiding foe with a Flare Arrow and shells their cover with Blast Arrows. Don't hide in one place.
  • Reach it on the battlements and it fights with the end of its bow.
  • Knocked off its tower, it is soon back at its post.

Drops

  • Marksman's Eye ×1
  • The Marksman's Recurve ×1 with Deadeye II
  • Bone ×1–3
  • Arrow ×2–6

The Ranger's Watchtower

A ruined stone-and-spruce tower, three floors and a battlemented top. It stands in every taiga, forest, flower forest, birch forest, old growth birch forest and dappled forest, but rarely. At most one stands in each 768 × 768-block region of the world, and only where that region's chosen spot is one of those biomes. Most regions have none, so the nearest is often a kilometre or more away. A chest on each floor holds arrows, Broadhead, Blunt and Rope Arrows, bread and the makings of more (flint, sticks, feathers, string, iron nuggets, leather), now and then a Quiver; one more waits on the battlements for whoever wins the duel, with Bodkin Arrows too. The Recurve Bow is the Marksman's own: it isn't chest loot.

Every spawn can be turned off in the server config; spawn eggs and commands still work.
