Modpacks and config

The server and client config, the tags and data a pack can change, and how Full Draw sits beside other mods.

Server config

NeoForge: config/fulldraw-synced.toml, sent to every player and re-sent when it changes. Fabric: config/fulldraw.properties, read at startup. The keys are the same on both.

Client config

Each player's own: what they see and hear. NeoForge config/fulldraw-client.toml, Fabric config/fulldraw-client.properties.

KeyDefaultWhat it does
showRingtrueThe draw ring, the hit tick and early/late around the crosshair.

fovEffectstrueBow zoom on the bows the shot rules cover, Steady Aim's narrower view and the release kick.
perfectTrailtrueThe particle trail behind Perfect arrows.
drawSoundstrueThe draw creak, the strain of a held draw, the twang on release and your own breath.
perfectChimetrueThe chime and the flash at the bow of your own Perfect Release.

Tags and data

Item tagHolds
#fulldraw:no_shot_pipelineWeapons Full Draw leaves alone: they shoot as their mod made them, don't draw from the Quiver and can't take Deadeye, Quickdraw or Steady. A bow or crossbow that overrides how it fires is left alone without the tag.
#fulldraw:quiver_ammoWhat a Quiver takes (by default #minecraft:arrows).
#fulldraw:shield_piercingWeapons whose bolts crack a Shieldbearer's shield (the Arbalest).
#fulldraw:loads_aloneAmmunition a magazine crossbow loads one at a time (the Blast Arrow and firework rockets).
#fulldraw:fletching/headsWhat the Fletching Table's head slot takes; also shafts, fletchings, extras.
Entity tagHolds
#fulldraw:cannot_bleedCreatures a Broadhead can't make bleed: skeletons, golems, slimes, the wither and others.

Full Draw's bows join #minecraft:enchantable/bow and #c:tools/bow, its crossbows #minecraft:enchantable/crossbow and #c:tools/crossbow, and its arrows #minecraft:arrows.

Weak spots

A creature's head is found from its eye height. To give one a different weak spot, add data/<namespace>/fulldraw/weak_spot/<entity>.json, with boxes in fractions of its bounding box (0 to 1 across, up and front to back; past the edges is fine) and an optional multiplier:

{"boxes": [{"min": [-1, 0.68, 0.4], "max": [2, 2, 2]}], "multiplier": 2.0}

Or {"none": true} for a creature with no weak spot, as the slime has.

Fletching recipes

Recipes of type fulldraw:fletching take head, shaft, fletching and an optional extra, each an ingredient with a count:

{
  "type": "fulldraw:fletching",
  "head": {
    "ingredient": "minecraft:iron_nugget",
    "count": 2
  },
  "shaft": {
    "ingredient": "minecraft:stick"
  },
  "fletching": {
    "ingredient": "minecraft:feather"
  },
  "result": {
    "id": "fulldraw:broadhead_arrow",
    "count": 8
  }
}

Each part must also be in its slot's tag (#fulldraw:fletching/heads and the others), or the slot won't take it; the server log names any recipe part a tag leaves out.

Switching things off

  • Where the creatures spawn: the biome tags #fulldraw:spawns/gale_harrier and #fulldraw:spawns/bog_lurker; override one with "replace": true and an empty values list and that creature spawns nowhere (without replace the empty list merges and changes nothing). The server config also has a switch for each of the three. On NeoForge the weights and group sizes are data too (fulldraw:neoforge/biome_modifier/spawns/*); on Fabric they are fixed (a weight of 10 for the Harrier, 20 for the Lurker).
  • The Ranger's Watchtower: empty #fulldraw:has_structure/rangers_watchtower, or override the structure set fulldraw:rangers_watchtower. As with the spawn tags, an emptied tag needs "replace": true.
  • The fletcher's trades: Full Draw puts its trades in vanilla's #minecraft:fletcher/level_2 to level_4 tags and raises those trade sets' amount from 2 to 3, so vanilla's trades stay. Removing Full Draw's entries from the tags and setting amount back to 2 restores vanilla's fletcher.
  • The Fletching Table: with no fulldraw:fletching recipe loaded it is vanilla's block again, with no menu.
  • The zoom: Full Draw zooms the view of every bow the shot rules cover, but vanilla's own, while it is drawn. A modded bow that zooms on its own then zooms twice: tag it #fulldraw:no_shot_pipeline, or a player can turn fovEffects off in their client config.

Other mods

  • Other mods' bows that fire as vanilla's does get the shot rules (Perfect window, headshots, range) at a vanilla bow's numbers, unless tagged out. A bow or crossbow that overrides how it fires (its release or its shot) gets neither the rules nor the Quiver's arrows. Crossbows, ours and theirs, have no Perfect window and their bolts take no head or range multiplier.
  • Other mods' creatures get a weak spot from their eye height.
  • Arrows from other mods go in the Quiver when they're in #minecraft:arrows.
  • The enchantments are ordinary data enchantments, so mods that list or move enchantments see them.
